**Real-World High-Impact Use Cases:**

**Tier 1 — Immediate SOC Value**
- **Alert Triage** — EDR/SIEM alert comes in, instantly maps to Kill Chain stage + D3FEND countermeasures. Cuts analyst triage time from 20 min to seconds
- **Defensive Gap Assessment** — Before a pentest or audit, run your deployed stack through gap analysis. Know your blind spots before the attacker does

**Tier 2 — Incident Response**
- **Live Incident Mapping** — During an active breach, track attacker progression across Kill Chain stages in real time using timeline events
- **Post-Incident Hardening** — After an incident, auto-map every used TTP to missing defensive controls. Turns IR reports into actionable roadmaps

**Tier 3 — Strategic**
- **Security Budgeting** — Show leadership exactly which D3FEND tactic categories lack coverage and why investment is needed. Evidence-based, not opinion-based

These 5 are defensible in front of a CISO, SOC manager, or IR team — every one of them solves a real pain point with a measurable outcome.
